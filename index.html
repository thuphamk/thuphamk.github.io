Executive Positioning

Not a specialist in one function. A leader who connects them.

Strategy only matters when it can be translated into execution. Marketing works better when it understands operations. Growth becomes sustainable when customers, people, products and processes move in the same direction.

My career has never followed a single function. I have worked across operations, commercial, marketing, customer experience, purchasing, product development and business management.

That experience shaped how I see business: not as separate departments, but as connected systems — where a pricing decision, a staffing model and a customer complaint are usually the same problem viewed from three different desks.
