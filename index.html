Executive Positioning

Not a specialist in one function. A leader who connects them.

Strategy only matters when it can be translated into execution. Marketing works better when it understands operations. Growth becomes sustainable when customers, people, products and processes move in the same direction.

My career has never followed a single function. I have worked across operations, commercial, marketing, customer experience, purchasing, product development and business management.

That experience shaped how I see business: not as separate departments, but as connected systems, where a decision in one function is rarely isolated from the rest.

About

I'm Thu.

On 25 July 2013 I opened my first store. A man came to congratulate me, looked around the shophouse and advised me to close it down. Two or three days later, a few acquaintances messaged to say the fruit they bought on opening day smelled off.

I didn't have a name for that then. In the years since, across logistics, commercial, marketing, product and now running a company, I keep meeting it again: a problem that shows up in one department usually starts in another.

I currently lead Saigon Security, I'm completing my MBA at HUTECH, and I hold a teaching-pedagogy certificate. I'd like to share what I've learned, including the times it didn't go well, with anyone it might help.
